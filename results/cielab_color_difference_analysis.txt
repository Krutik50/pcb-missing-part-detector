===============================================================================================
ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS WHY CIELAB DELTA-E FAILS:
===============================================================================================
1. Local Glare Non-Uniformity: A specular highlight spot (e.g. on calib_06 or calib_07)
   is highly localized. Global substrate normalization cannot remove localized glare beams,
   causing present parts under glare to suffer massive Delta E spikes (Delta E > 25).
2. High Missing Footprint Color Camouflage: Dark components (like SOIC8 EEPROM or QFP32 body)
   rest on dark green substrate. When missing, the dark substrate exposed underneath has very
   low Delta E relative to the dark plastic IC package (Delta E < 10 in some cases).
3. Contrast Invariance Loss: ZNCC measures spatial shape / gradient cross-correlation,
   which is robust to absolute color shifts. CIELAB Delta E is purely an absolute color distance
   and lacks spatial pattern recognition.
