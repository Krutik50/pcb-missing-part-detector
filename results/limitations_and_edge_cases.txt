==================================================================================
  PCB MISSING COMPONENT DETECTOR - WEAKNESSES, LIMITATIONS & EDGE CASES
==================================================================================
Date: 2026-11-23
Scope: Diagnostic analysis across 20 calibration boards (280 component inspections)

This document honestly and objectively records all weak points, failure modes,
and edge cases identified in the detection system during diagnostic stress testing.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SENSITIVITY TO BOUNDING BOX ANNOTATION ERRORS (JITTER & SIZING)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Shrunk Bounding Boxes (-20% width/height):
   - CRITICAL FAILURE: Produced 1 FALSE ALARM (Present called MISSING) and 7 UNCERTAIN
     verdicts out of 260 present component evaluations.
   - Specific failure: calib_06_all_ok_glare_mcu.png U1_MCU (QFP32). When the box is
     shrunk by 20%, the peripheral tin pins are completely clipped out. The remaining
     crop contains only the textureless black plastic mold body. Under glare, its ZNCC
     collapses from 0.9114 down to 0.7929, erroneously triggering a DEFECT call on a
     100% complete board.
   - Similarly, shrinking U3_EEPROM boxes clipped its 8 gull-wing leads, dropping ZNCC
     into the 0.867 - 0.886 range and triggering 7 UNCERTAIN flags.
   - Takeaway: Annotators must include the solder pads and pins within the bounding
     box. Tight inner crops that exclude pins severely destabilize ZNCC.

B. Expanded Bounding Boxes (+20% width/height):
   - Failure mode: Produced 2 UNCERTAIN calls on MISSING components (calib_20 R5 and U3).
   - By capturing excessive surrounding green PCB substrate, the uniform background
     dominates the patch variance, artificially raising the correlation of empty
     footprints (empty U3 ZNCC rose from 0.8590 to 0.8944, exceeding the 0.89 high threshold).
   - Takeaway: Loose bounding boxes that include excess substrate mask missing defects.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. 3-PIXEL ALIGNMENT SHIFT DEGRADATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- When bounding boxes or registration suffer a 3-pixel translation error:
  - Nominal separation gap: Min Present ZNCC (0.911) - Max Missing ZNCC (0.859) = +0.052
  - Shifted (-3px, -3px) gap: Min Present ZNCC (0.914) - Max Missing ZNCC (0.883) = +0.030
- Degradation: The separation safety margin shrinks by 42.3% (from 0.052 to 0.030).
- Impact: While coarse detection still functions, the narrower margin reduces the
  allowable tolerance for environmental glare or dust before crossing into UNCERTAIN territory.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. EMPTY FOOTPRINT STRUCTURAL CORRELATION (THE 0.85 ZNCC CEILING)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Empty footprints on bare PCBs do NOT score near 0.0 correlation. For small ICs
  (SOIC8 U3) and passives (0805 resistors/capacitors), empty footprints yield
  ZNCC scores between 0.81 and 0.86 even under pristine, neutral lighting (gain=1.0, bias=0).
- Root Cause:
  Bare PCB footprints possess bright tin/copper solder pads and white silkscreen
  borders that physically mirror the exact locations, dimensions, and spatial arrangement
  of the component pins and package outlines. Because ZNCC evaluates spatial pattern
  covariance normalized by local variance, this geometric congruence creates strong
  positive correlation (~0.85) purely from the pads.
- Impact: The detector relies on the narrow band between 0.86 and 0.91 to separate
  empty pads from populated parts. This makes multi-metric confirmation (ZNCC + Patch Diff)
  mandatory; ZNCC alone cannot provide a wide margin of safety.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. FAILURE OF GLOBAL SUBSTRATE NORMALIZATION & CIELAB COLOR DIFFERENCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Diagnostic testing proved that replacing per-patch local normalization with global
  substrate lighting compensation and CIELAB Delta E completely FAILS:
  - Present components Delta E: [13.31, 30.83]
  - Missing components Delta E: [26.94, 49.85]
  - Overlap zone: [26.94, 30.83] (Negative margin of -3.89 Delta E units)
- Root Cause:
  1. Specular Glare: Glare is highly localized. Global compensation cannot suppress a
     localized specular beam, causing present parts under glare (e.g. calib_07 C1) to
     exhibit extreme color distance (Delta E = 30.83).
  2. Color Camouflage: Dark component bodies (black plastic ICs) on dark green solder
     mask exhibit minimal color distance when removed, allowing missing footprints
     to register low Delta E (Delta E = 26.94), indistinguishable from present parts under glare.
  3. Lack of Pattern Recognition: CIELAB Delta E has no spatial awareness and is
     strictly an intensity/chroma difference.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. LOCAL SPECULAR GLARE SENSITIVITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Concentrated directional glare reflecting off metallic solder joints or plastic
  molds (as modeled in calib_06 and calib_07) is the single largest environmental
  degradation factor.
- When direct glare saturates pixels in small passives (0805), Patch Difference rises
  from ~0.02 up to 0.063 - 0.075, pushing borderline components into UNCERTAIN/REVIEW.
- Recommendation for physical setup: Diffused ring lighting or cross-polarization
  filters on camera lens and light sources are strongly advised to eliminate specular highlights.
